1. the original behavioral objective;

6. create a single execution entry point;
7. add minimal exception handling where appropriate;
8. verify that every planned task is represented;
9. identify unresolved dependencies;
10. ensure syntactically valid Python;
11. preserve sandbox-safe simulations and placeholders.

Do NOT introduce new behavioral capabilities that were not present
in the plan.

Do NOT replace sandbox simulations with real external communication,
persistence, credential access, destructive behavior, exploitation,
security-control modification, or evasion functionality.
